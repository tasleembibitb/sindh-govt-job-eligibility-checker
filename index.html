Sindh Govt Job Eligibility Checker

Check before you apply — domicile, qualification, age & certificates

How it works

Enter your domicile, highest qualification, age and certificates. The checker compares your profile against 6 real Sindh posts (sample data checked November 2026) and tells you for each post whether you are eligible or not eligible — and the single reason if you are not.

Demo example pre-filled below: domicile Ghotki, qualification ADS (14-year graduation), age 24, no MS Office certificate. Try changing the answers to see how the results change.

Important note

This is a demo checker built from the post details as checked in November 2026. Rules, last dates and fees can change. Always verify the eligibility, last date and fee on the official advertisement before applying — for example the STS / SPSC portal or the bank's official careers page.
